About

Building translational, practical, and explainable AI for clinical medicine.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Hwiyoung Kim and Prof. Yujin Oh. I build clinical AI that is translational, practical to deploy, and able to explain itself to the clinicians who use it. My work spans computational pathology and, increasingly, multi-agent LLM systems that reason jointly over medical images, structured clinical records, laboratory values, genomic profiles, and free-text reports.

Concretely, I work on multiple instance learning (MIL) and self-supervised pre-training for whole slide image (WSI) analysis, and on LLM-agent architectures for clinical decision support — with a recurring focus on settings where labels are scarce or the data distribution shifts across institutions. One question runs through all of it: given a gigapixel pathology slide or a full ICU record, can a model point to which inputs grounded its prediction, and does that grounding hold up in real clinical settings?
